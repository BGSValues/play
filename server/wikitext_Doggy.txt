The '''Doggy''' is a Common [[Pets|pet]] in Bubble Gum Simulator. It can be obtained by opening a [[Common Egg]]. The chance of hatching it is '''40%'''.

It can also be obtained by purchasing the '''198th''' slot in the [[Sea Rewards]].

== '''Trivia''' ==
* This pet is also a part of the [[Dogcat]], [[Classic Trio]], [[Split]], [[Gloom Split]], and [[Tophat (A)]].
* This is the weakest pet in the game.
