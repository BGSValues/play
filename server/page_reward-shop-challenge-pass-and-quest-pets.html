BGS Collab Value List

➢ Blue/Cyan Text = Pet's Value is in Secrets Value!

➢ Yellow/Orange Text = Pet's Value is in Shiny/Normal Value!

➢ Every pet from updates 33 and before (2019 Halloween and before) shown as Level 25 Enchant 40

➢ Every pet from updates 36 to 61 (500M Event to Fall 2020) shown as Level 25 Enchant 50

➢ Every pet from updates 62 to the current update (Halloween 2020 and after) shown as Level 25 Bubble Enchant

➢ If you click/tap on the item's name, it'll bring you to the BGS Wiki page about it. You'll see it's stats, chances, and more there.

➢ Demand System is out of 11 (0-11). 0 Means the item doesn't exist, 11 means the item's demand is hyped (a new item). 1 Means terrible demand, 10 means high demand.

➢ Stability: ⬆⬆ = rising fast ⬆ = rising ↔ = stable 🔄 = unstable ⬇ = dropping ⬇⬇ = dropping fast

➢ If an item is 500% and 5/10 demand, then it gets 500% in 5/10 demand items. It could get 600% in 3/10 demand items. It could also get 400% in 7/10 demand items. (This is just an example)
