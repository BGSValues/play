{{Pets_Infobox|image1 = Godly Gem.png|image2 = }}The '''Godly Gem''' is a [[Secret Pets|secret]] Legendary [[:Category:Pets|pet]] in Bubble Gum Simulator. It could have been obtained by opening a [[Heavenly Egg]]. The chance of hatching it was 1 in 100 million '''(0.000001%)''', or 1 in 50 million '''(0.000002%)''' with the {{RLinkGP|5446881|Lucky Chances}} [[Gamepasses|gamepass]].
