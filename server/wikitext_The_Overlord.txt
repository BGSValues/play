{{Pets Infobox|image1 = The Overlord.png|image2 = Shiny The Overlord.png|image3 = Mythic The Overlord.png}}'''The Overlord''' is a [[:Category:Secret Pets|secret]] Legendary [[:Category:Pets|pet]] in Bubble Gum Simulator. It can be obtained by opening a [[Nightmare Egg]]. The chance of hatching it is 1 in 4 million '''(0.000025%)''', or 1 in 2 million '''(0.00005%)''' with the {{RLinkGP|5446881|Lucky Chances}} [[Gamepasses|gamepass]].

== Trivia ==
* This was the best pet in the game until the [[Festivus]] came out in Update 4.
* This pet was first hinted at on Isaac’s tweet on [[Category:Articles using external links]] [https://twitter.com/IsaacRBLX Twitter] saying, “And you thought there were only 9 pets in this new egg....”
* As of Update 82, this pet has 25 reskins, [[2018 Overlord]], [[Jelly Overlord]], [[The OwOlord]], [[Gummy Overlord]], [[Diamond Overlord]], [[Lucky Overlord]], [[Queen Overlord]], [[Atlantis Overlord]], [[Platinum Overlord]], [[Rainbow Overlord]], [[Dual Lord]], [[Dark Lord]], [[Easter Overlord]], [[Sakuralord]], [[Gem Overlord]], [[Citrus Overlord]], [[Slime Overlord]], [[Candycorn Lord]], [[Ghostlord]], [[Ice Overlord]],[[2020 Overlord]], [[Golflord]], [[Shadow Overlord]], [[Infernolord]], and [[Alien Overlord]].
* This is the only secret pet to not have a 4th multiplier.
* In Update 44, this pet's size was increased due to previously being considerably smaller than other [[:Category:Secret Items|secret pets]] that came in the later updates.
* In update 81, this pet, along with multiple other permanent [[:Category:Secret Items|secret pets]] were given mythic variants. This was apart of the first wave of old permanent secrets getting mythic variants.
